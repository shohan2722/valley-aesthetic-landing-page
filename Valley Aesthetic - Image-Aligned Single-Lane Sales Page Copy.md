## Do I need to know which treatment I want before contacting you?

No.

Tell us what you want to improve. Our team can help determine which treatment options make sense based on your concern and eligibility.

## Are the introductory offers real?

Yes. Valley Aesthetic periodically offers introductory pricing on selected treatments for eligible patients.

We’ll confirm the treatment, offer details, and eligibility with you before you book.

## How many treatments will I need?

That depends entirely on what is being treated, the treatment selected, and your starting point.

Some people notice improvement after an initial treatment. Other concerns are better addressed through a series.

Your consultation is where we’ll give you a realistic recommendation.

## Will I have downtime?

It depends on the treatment.

Some aesthetic treatments involve little or no downtime, while others may cause temporary redness, sensitivity, swelling, or other short-term effects.

We’ll explain this before you decide to proceed.

## Will I still look like myself?

That’s the goal.

Our philosophy is improvement, not reinvention.

We want the result to look appropriate for you.

You’ll start a private conversation with the Valley Aesthetic team.

We’ll ask what you’d like to improve, answer your initial questions, confirm any relevant offer details, and determine the next step.

No obligation. No pressure.
