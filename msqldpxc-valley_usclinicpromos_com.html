Frequently Asked Questions

  • What’s the cost?

    Our comprehensive 3-in-1 Body Sculpting Treatment is just $129!

  • How long does each treatment take?

    Each session lasts only 20 to 40 minutes!

  • When will I notice results?

    You may start seeing results as early as your first session! Some clients experience up to a 1-inch reduction after just one Cryoskin® treatment.

  • How many treatments will I need?

    Typically, around 5 sessions are recommended for optimal results, although this can vary depending on individual goals.

  • How does the treatment work?

    This non-invasive technique uses a combination of heating and cooling, delivered through a specialized massaging wand, to target and eliminate fat cells. It's designed to reduce cellulite, sculpt, and tone stubborn areas, all without any downtime.

    Cryoskin® utilizes cryolipolysis, a scientific process where fat cells are destroyed through controlled temperature changes and naturally eliminated via the lymphatic system.

  • Is the treatment painful?

    Not at all! Our massaging technique is actually quite soothing, and the alternating heat and cold sensations contribute to a relaxing experience with minimal discomfort.

  • How does Cryoskin® differ from CoolSculpting?

    CoolSculpting is often associated with discomfort, pain, bruising, and potential risks for some individuals.

    Unlike CoolSculpting, Cryoskin® allows precise targeting of specific areas for more effective body sculpting. Plus, results can be seen in as little as 15 days to 3 weeks, compared to the 2-3 month waiting period with CoolSculpting. Cryoskin® is also more affordable, safer, and a more pleasant experience overall.

  • Are the results permanent?

    Yes, the results are permanent. Once fat cells are destroyed through Cryoskin®, they are gone for good and are naturally eliminated from your body.

    To maintain the best appearance, some clients opt for occasional follow-up sessions, but this is entirely optional.

  • How should I get ready for a session?

    No special preparation is needed, as the treatment is non-invasive with zero downtime. For optimal results, try to avoid consuming sugar for 2 hours before your session and skip working out immediately before and after treatment. Otherwise, you're good to go!
