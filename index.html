The Cryoskin approach

A More Targeted Approach to Body Contouring.

Cryoskin uses controlled temperature changes delivered through a specialized treatment wand. The applicator is moved across the treatment area while alternating temperatures are used as part of the body-contouring process.

The experience is completely non-invasive. No needles. No incisions. And no recovery period that forces you to rearrange your week.

Frequently asked questions

Start With the Details.

What exactly is included in the $129 offer?

The introductory offer includes 1 Cryoskin Slimming Treatment, 1 Cryoskin Facial, and 1 Skin Analysis. The regular stated value is $550. Your intro price is $129.

Is Cryoskin invasive?

No. Cryoskin is performed externally using a handheld treatment applicator. There are no injections or surgical incisions involved.

Is there downtime?

Cryoskin is promoted as a no-downtime treatment, so you can generally return to your normal activities afterward.

What does the treatment feel like?

Cryoskin involves changing temperatures while the applicator is moved across the treatment area. Many clients find the treatment manageable and some describe the massaging process as relaxing.

Can I see a difference after one treatment?

Some clients may notice a visible change after an initial session. However, everyone’s body and goals are different. Your results cannot be predicted from someone else’s before-and-after photo.

Will I need more than one treatment?

Possibly. A series may be recommended depending on your goals and response. The $129 offer lets you experience Cryoskin before deciding whether additional sessions make sense.

Is Cryoskin a weight-loss treatment?

No. Think of Cryoskin as a targeted aesthetic/body-contouring treatment rather than a substitute for weight loss.

Can anyone receive Cryoskin?

Not necessarily. Treatment eligibility depends on the individual. The Valley Aesthetic team should confirm that Cryoskin is appropriate for you before treatment.

What happens after I claim the offer?

Follow the booking steps and the Valley Aesthetic team will help confirm the offer, discuss the area you’d like to target, check eligibility, and arrange your appointment.
